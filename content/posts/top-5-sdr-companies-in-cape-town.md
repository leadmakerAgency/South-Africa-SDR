**Introduction** 

Outsourced SDR companies in Cape Town combine multi-channel prospecting including cold email, LinkedIn outreach, and phone execution with structured sales playbooks to build predictable pipeline engines. Below are five top specialized SDR providers operating directly out of Cape Town.

1. South Africa SDR ([https://www.southafricasdr.com/](https://www.southafricasdr.com/)) 

South Africa SDR ranks as the premier specialist dedicated strictly to outbound sales development and sales force augmentation. Operating out of Cape Town, they deploy fully managed, dedicated SDR pods tailored for B2B tech, SaaS, enterprise, and corporate service providers to deliver verified, high-intent meeting bookings directly into client CRMs.

2. Delta-v ([https://deltav-global.com/](https://deltav-global.com/)) 

Based in Claremont, Cape Town, Delta-v is a dedicated sales development agency that builds and manages embedded SDR pods for fast-growing B2B, FinTech, and software companies. Each pod is paired with a dedicated Sales Development Manager and custom outbound playbooks, helping global tech teams scale high-value discovery meetings seamlessly across European and US time zones.

3. Persuade ([https://www.persuade.co.za/](https://www.persuade.co.za/)) 

Operating from Cape Town, Persuade builds end-to-end outbound revenue infrastructure for mid-market and enterprise B2B organizations. Their specialized SDRs execute multi-channel plays combining intent data, precision list architecture, personalized cold email, and phone outreach to connect with senior decision-makers across the UK, Europe, and Middle East.

4. SDR Agency ([https://www.sdragency.co.za/](https://www.sdragency.co.za/)) 

Located in Bellville Park, Cape Town, SDR Agency delivers outsourced sales development and lead qualification frameworks for B2B companies. They specialize in cold email campaigns, LinkedIn prospecting, and targeted phone outreach, designing custom sales playbooks and enablement tools to drive consistent pipeline growth.

5. MANGO5 ([https://mango5.co.za/](https://mango5.co.za/)) 

Headquartered in Cape Town, MANGO5 is an established BPO and outbound sales support provider. They provide fully trained outbound SDR and business development reps who handle cold prospecting, top-of-funnel screening, and appointment setting for UK, US, and international sales teams.
