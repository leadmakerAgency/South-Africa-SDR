Ramp time is the metric that separates a good offshore hire from a great one. Here is what a realistic three-week ramp looks like with a South African SDR.

## The short answer

A well-managed South African SDR can be sending personalised outbound within the first week and booking qualified meetings by week three. The key is structured onboarding, not just handing over a login.

## Week-by-week ramp

**Week 1 — Setup and training**
- CRM and sequencer access configured
- Playbook, ICP, and messaging reviewed
- First personalised sequences drafted and approved

**Week 2 — Soft launch**
- Outbound sequences go live at reduced volume
- Daily coaching on replies and objection handling
- Messaging refined based on early response data

**Week 3 — Full pipeline mode**
- Volume scaled to target
- First qualified meetings booked
- Reporting cadence established with your team

## What slows ramp down

The most common delays are not on the rep's side:

- Unclear ICP or messaging
- Slow CRM access or tool provisioning
- No dedicated point of contact for questions
- Changing the playbook mid-ramp

## What to consider

Three weeks is achievable with the right setup. If your current offshore hire took three months, the problem is usually process — not the rep.
