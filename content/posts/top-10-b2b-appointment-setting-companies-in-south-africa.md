**Introduction**

South Africa has become a leading international hub for outbound sales development and Business Process Outsourcing (BPO). By leveraging structured multi-channel outreach combining phone prospecting, email sequences, and social selling—specialized South African agencies qualify leads and book high-intent discovery calls directly onto account executive calendars. The ten agencies below represent the top B2B appointment setting providers operating out of the region.

1. [South Africa SDR](https://www.southafricasdr.com/)


South Africa SDR ranks as the premier specialist in dedicated outbound sales development and sales force augmentation in the country. Operating out of Cape Town, they focus on deploying fully managed, dedicated Sales Development Representative (SDR) pods tailored specifically for B2B tech, SaaS, enterprise, and corporate service providers. Combining cold email outreach, LinkedIn prospecting, and phone calling, South Africa SDR builds bespoke contact lists mapped to target decision-makers to systematically deliver verified, high-intent meeting bookings.

2. [Persuade](https://persuade.co.za/)


Operating out of Cape Town, Persuade specializes in high-ticket B2B lead generation and targeted account-based marketing (ABM). They focus on complex industries such as technology, compliance, and financial services, utilizing multi-channel outreach to schedule direct meetings with senior corporate decision-makers.

3. [Afrishore BPO](https://afrishorebpo.com/)


Afrishore BPO provides end-to-end B2B outbound prospecting and inbound lead qualification. Serving clients across marketing, technology, and financial sectors, their dedicated sales development teams manage top-of-funnel engagement and CRM updating to help client sales teams scale meeting volume smoothly.

4. [MANGO5](https://mango5.co.za/)


Based in Cape Town, MANGO5 is a well-established contact center provider offering scalable outbound appointment setting and lead qualification. Primarily catering to B2B clients in the UK, US, and local markets, they combine rigorous agent training programs with multi-touch outreach strategies to drive steady pipeline growth.

5. [Shift One Digital](https://shiftone.co.za/)

Shift One Digital integrates full-funnel digital marketing with specialized outbound B2B appointment setting. Their team executes target market research, tailored script design, and personalized outreach across email and phone to engage key decision-makers across sectors like tech, manufacturing, and professional services.

6. [VA Connect](https://vaconnect.co.za/)

VA Connect provides dedicated virtual sales development representatives (SDRs) for growing businesses and mid-market enterprises. Their representatives manage cross-channel prospecting via LinkedIn, email, and cold outreach, providing a flexible framework to expand pipeline reach without increasing internal overhead.

7. [ARC Business Process Outsourcing](https://www.arcbpo.com/)

Operating out of Durban, ARC Business Process Outsourcing provides structured outbound telemarketing and appointment setting solutions. ARC helps businesses manage labor-intensive top-of-funnel prospecting tasks by screening contact databases, identifying operational decision-makers, and qualifying leads prior to account executive hand-offs.

8. [The Cold Calling Company](https://www.coldcallingcompany.co.za/)


The Cold Calling Company is a South Africa-based B2B outbound sales agency focused specifically on phone-driven appointment setting. They build targeted prospect lists, bypass gatekeepers, and engage decision-makers through direct conversations. Their execution model eliminates the need for internal SDR hiring while providing predictable meeting volume.

9. [The Agentsy BPO](https://www.agentsybpo.com/)


The Agentsy BPO places dedicated, offshore South African appointment setters and sales development talent into international growth teams. Aligning with European, UK, and US time zones, their outbound reps execute structured cold email, phone, and social selling sequences to book meetings directly into client CRMs.

10. [Outsource-X](https://outsource-x.com/)

Based in Somerset West, Outsource-X provides sales process outsourcing, data enrichment, and top-of-funnel appointment setting. They specialize in multi-channel prospecting and initial account verification, allowing client sales teams to focus strictly on closing qualified opportunities.
