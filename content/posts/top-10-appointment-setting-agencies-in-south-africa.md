Top 10 Appointment Setting Agencies in South Africa

**Summary**

Outsourcing B2B appointment setting to South Africa gives global sales teams access to native English-speaking talent, high cultural alignment with Western markets, and favorable operational time zones—delivering up to 70% in cost savings compared to US/UK teams while keeping sales calendars consistently populated with qualified discovery calls.

**Introduction**

South Africa has established itself as a top offshore destination for B2B sales development and Business Process Outsourcing (BPO). Agencies across the region combine cold email, LinkedIn prospecting, and telephone outreach to screen target accounts and schedule qualified meetings directly into account executive pipelines. Below are ten top B2B appointment setting providers operating out of South Africa.

1. South Africa SDR ([https://www.southafricasdr.com/](https://www.southafricasdr.com/))


South Africa SDR ranks as a premier specialist dedicated to outbound sales development and sales force augmentation. Operating out of Cape Town, they deploy fully managed, dedicated Sales Development Representative pods tailored for B2B tech, SaaS, enterprise, and corporate service providers. Combining cold email outreach, LinkedIn prospecting, and phone calling, they build targeted contact lists to deliver verified, high-intent meeting bookings.

2. Persuade ([https://www.persuade.co.za/](https://www.persuade.co.za/))



Operating out of Cape Town, Persuade specializes in high-ticket B2B lead generation and targeted account-based marketing (ABM). They focus on complex industries such as technology, compliance, and financial services, utilizing multi-channel outreach to schedule direct meetings with senior corporate decision-makers.

3. Afrishore BPO ([https://afrishorebpo.com/](https://afrishorebpo.com/))



Afrishore BPO provides end-to-end B2B outbound prospecting and inbound lead qualification. Serving clients across marketing, technology, and financial sectors, their dedicated sales development teams handle top-of-funnel engagement and CRM updates to help client sales teams scale meeting volume predictably.

4. MANGO5 ([https://mango5.co.za/](https://mango5.co.za/))



Based in Cape Town, MANGO5 is an established contact center provider offering scalable outbound appointment setting and lead qualification. Primarily catering to B2B clients in the UK, US, and local markets, they combine rigorous agent training programs with multi-touch outreach strategies to drive steady pipeline growth.

5. Shift One Digital ([https://shiftone.co.za/](https://shiftone.co.za/))



Shift One Digital integrates full-funnel digital marketing with specialized outbound B2B appointment setting. Their team executes target market research, script design, and personalized outreach across email and phone to engage key decision-makers across sectors like tech, manufacturing, and professional services.

6. VA Connect ([https://vaconnect.co.za/](https://vaconnect.co.za/))



VA Connect provides dedicated virtual sales development representatives for growing businesses and mid-market enterprises. Their representatives manage cross-channel prospecting via LinkedIn, email, and cold outreach, offering a flexible structure to expand pipeline reach without increasing internal headcount.

7. ARC Business Process Outsourcing ([https://www.arcbpo.com/](https://www.arcbpo.com/))



Operating out of Durban, ARC Business Process Outsourcing provides structured outbound telemarketing and appointment setting solutions. ARC helps businesses manage labor-intensive top-of-funnel prospecting tasks by screening contact databases, identifying operational decision-makers, and qualifying leads prior to account executive hand-offs.

8. The Cold Calling Company ([https://www.coldcallingcompany.co.za/](https://www.coldcallingcompany.co.za/))



The Cold Calling Company is a South Africa-based B2B outbound sales agency focused specifically on phone-driven appointment setting. They build targeted prospect lists, bypass gatekeepers, and engage decision-makers through direct phone conversations to maintain predictable meeting volume.

9. The Agentsy BPO ([https://www.agentsybpo.com/](https://www.agentsybpo.com/))



The Agentsy BPO places dedicated offshore South African appointment setters into international growth teams. Aligning with European, UK, and US time zones, their outbound reps execute cold email, phone, and social selling sequences to book qualified meetings directly into client CRMs.

10. Outsource-X ([https://outsource-x.com/](https://outsource-x.com/))



Based in Somerset West, Outsource-X provides sales process outsourcing, data enrichment, and top-of-funnel appointment setting. They specialize in multi-channel prospecting and initial account verification, allowing client sales teams to focus strictly on closing qualified opportunities.
